# Orbital Speed of the ISS vs Earth's Orbital Speed Around the Sun

## Question

Calculate the orbital speed of the International Space Station (ISS), which orbits at an altitude of approximately $400\ \text{km}$ above the Earth's surface. Given that the Earth's mass is

$$
M_E \approx 5.97 \times 10^{24}\ \text{kg},
$$

compare this speed with Earth's orbital speed around the Sun, using:

- Earth–Sun distance:

$$
150 \times 10^6\ \text{km}
$$

- Earth's orbital period:

$$
365.25\ \text{days}
$$

Which is faster: the ISS orbiting the Earth or the Earth orbiting the Sun?

---

## Solution

### 1. Orbital Speed of the ISS

For a circular orbit, the orbital speed is

$$
v = \sqrt{\frac{GM}{r}}
$$

where:

- $G = 6.674 \times 10^{-11}\ \text{m}^3\text{kg}^{-1}\text{s}^{-2}$
- $M_E = 5.97 \times 10^{24}\ \text{kg}$
- Earth's radius:

$$
R_E \approx 6.37 \times 10^6\ \text{m}
$$

- ISS altitude:

$$
h = 400\ \text{km} = 4.00 \times 10^5\ \text{m}
$$

The orbital radius is

$$
r = R_E + h
$$

$$
r = 6.37 \times 10^6 + 4.00 \times 10^5
= 6.77 \times 10^6\ \text{m}
$$

Substituting into the orbital speed formula:

$$
v_{\text{ISS}}
=
\sqrt{
\frac{(6.674 \times 10^{-11})(5.97 \times 10^{24})}
{6.77 \times 10^6}
}
$$

$$
v_{\text{ISS}}
=
\sqrt{5.89 \times 10^7}
$$

$$
v_{\text{ISS}}
\approx 7.67 \times 10^3\ \text{m/s}
$$

$$
\boxed{v_{\text{ISS}} \approx 7.7\ \text{km/s}}
$$

---

### 2. Earth's Orbital Speed Around the Sun

The orbital speed is

$$
v = \frac{2\pi r}{T}
$$

where:

- Earth–Sun distance:

$$
r = 150 \times 10^6\ \text{km}
= 1.50 \times 10^{11}\ \text{m}
$$

- Orbital period:

$$
T = 365.25 \times 24 \times 3600
= 3.156 \times 10^7\ \text{s}
$$

Substituting:

$$
v_{\oplus}
=
\frac{2\pi(1.50 \times 10^{11})}
{3.156 \times 10^7}
$$

$$
v_{\oplus}
\approx 2.99 \times 10^4\ \text{m/s}
$$

$$
\boxed{v_{\oplus} \approx 29.9\ \text{km/s}}
$$

---

### 3. Comparison

$$
v_{\text{ISS}} \approx 7.7\ \text{km/s}
$$

$$
v_{\oplus} \approx 29.9\ \text{km/s}
$$

The ratio is

$$
\frac{v_{\oplus}}{v_{\text{ISS}}}
=
\frac{29.9}{7.7}
\approx 3.9
$$

Thus, Earth moves around the Sun about **3.9 times faster** than the ISS moves around Earth.

---

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{v_{\text{ISS}} \approx 7.7\ \text{km/s}}
$$

$$
\boxed{v_{\oplus} \approx 29.9\ \text{km/s}}
$$

Therefore,

$$
\boxed{\text{Earth's orbital speed around the Sun is greater than the ISS's orbital speed around Earth.}}
$$

Earth travels about **4 times faster** around the Sun than the ISS travels around Earth.
